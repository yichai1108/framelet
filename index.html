片言菲林 · FRAMELET

把片言，留在时光里。 Keep each fragment in the light of time.

我总迷信刹那。迷信快门“咔嗒”一声截断的时间流，迷信钢笔尖在纸面短暂驻足的凹痕。 这些零星的、未及修饰的显影，或许比过度曝光的回忆更值得留恋。

I have always placed my faith in the instant: in the click of a shutter severing the current of time, in the small indentation left when a fountain-pen nib pauses on paper. These scattered, unretouched impressions may be more worth holding on to than memories washed out by overexposure.

刘李 · Nate Lew Lee
